# The Central Sea

The central sea lies between the continents and contains several notable features.

## The Crystal Eye

A jutting spike of rock rises from the ocean, with a crystalline feature at its summit that catches and refracts sunlight in all directions. Surrounding it is a coral reef of extraordinary danger — so voracious it consumes any sea creature that approaches. A subspecies has evolved there, immune to the reef's poisons. The entire biodome is littered with the corpses of those who came too close.

## The Lavender Island

An island in the northern central sea features a striking lavender forest.

## The Inland Sea

On the eastern continent's western flank sits the largest inland sea (or massive lake) on the planet. Its shores include the home region of the [[Tubari]] and areas of rich, unsettled agricultural soil.

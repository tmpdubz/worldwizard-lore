# Central Continent

The central continent sits in the northern hemisphere between the western and eastern continents — a large, largely wild and undiscovered landmass. Much of its interior remains unmapped.

## Known Interior Features

- Rich agricultural lands
- Northern tundra
- **The Nipple of the North** — a very large hole. Its depth is unknown. No one knows what is at the bottom, or whether it has one.
- A blood sap forest (nature unknown; open to interpretation)
- At the southwestern edge, an impassable river separates the central continent from the eastern continent — a torrent of rough water, cascading rapids, and waterfalls that has never been successfully crossed

## The Southern Peninsula

The exception to the continent's wilderness is a peninsula jutting south. Its entire coastline consists of sheer, sun-bleached Mediterranean-style cliffs dotted with sea caves, coves, palm trees, and beaches.

This is the homeland of the [[Erisians]] and the location of their carved city [[Kildare]].

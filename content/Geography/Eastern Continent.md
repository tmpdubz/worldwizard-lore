# Eastern Continent

The eastern continent runs roughly north to south, with two major appendages: a southeastern peninsula (homeland of the [[Illakamaq]]) and a northeastern island cluster (home to the [[Verdant Ascendancy]], the [[Kanza]], and the original homeland of the [[Hephari]]).

## The World Cliff

The continent's most dramatic feature is the **World Cliff** — a continental escarpment of incomprehensible scale, kilometres tall, dominating the north. Warm air crashes against its face, feeding a vertical river that carves caves and entire biomes into the cliff wall. No one knows what lies atop the World Cliff.

## The Dank River of the North

East of the World Cliff, a hot spring aquifer bubbles up from the ground — putrid, sulphuric, and tainted. The surrounding area features rock shrubs (organic formations that grow slowly into rock trees) and a fungal forest. This is now the homeland of the corrupted [[Hephari]].

## The Sand Seas

South of the Dank River, a vast region of smooth pea-gravel desert gives way to the **Sand Seas** — bodies of sand that flow like water, with waves as high as a house. Homeland of the [[Kharakai]] and the site of [[Ssarqeth]].

## The Great Desert and Water Tree Forest

Further south, a massive desert of rolling dunes stretches across the continent. At its heart stands a water tree forest — a strange and poorly understood phenomenon. The desert is bounded to the south by an immense Himalaya-like mountain range.

## The Watu's Jungle Homeland

South of the mountain barrier lies the [[Watu]]'s mountainous jungle homeland, lush and temple-dotted. Their city [[Hekatu]] sits here. The Watu expanded southeast into swampier jungle territory. To the east of the Watu, a massive mangrove forest lines the coast.

## The Haunted Forest

Immediately south of the Watu's homeland lies an ancient forest haunted by the ghosts of the [[Exmara]] — the warlike orcish and goblinoid peoples who once lived there.

## The Wasteland and the Illakamaq Peninsula

Southeast of the Watu, glowing green rocks begin. This was once the homeland of the Cuttle Consortia (now extinct), and the radioactive legacy of their land warps and mutates those who linger. The wasteland narrows into an isthmus connecting to the [[Illakamaq]] peninsula — a stormy, volcanic region of extreme gravity distortion, marshlands, crystal canyons, and fungal forests. The peninsula's volcano erupted during the Prehistoric Age, reshaping the entire region.

## The Northeast Appendage

North and east of the World Cliff plateau:

- **Dead forests** near the sky islands, where dead trees jut from the ocean depths; they blend into living forests and then grasslands heading north
- **The floating islands** — home of the [[Verdant Ascendancy]]; solidified porous stone lifted by trapped gases, rising a kilometre above the ground
- **The Kanza Fjords** — an island of massive Scandinavian-scale fjords; homeland of the [[Kanza]]; location of [[Holen]]
- **The Hephari Plateau** — an island of ancient eroded flat-top mountains, perpetually cloudy, with rivers of fog spilling over ridges. Covered in old growth forest to the south, fungal forest to the north. The original homeland of the [[Hephari]] before they died and were reborn corrupted.

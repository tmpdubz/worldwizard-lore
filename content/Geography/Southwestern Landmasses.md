# Southwestern Landmasses

The southwestern region consists of two main landmasses: the southern tip of the western continent (homeland of the [[Lethari]]) and the large island called Thalrûnkar (homeland of the [[Thalrûn]]).

## Southern Tip of the Western Continent

Moving north to south:

1. **The Plains** — large grassy plains
2. **The Great Delta** — a massive river delta fed by hundreds of small rivers draining into the ocean. The rivers run through a vast boreal forest of super-tall coniferous trees. This is the homeland of the [[Lethari]] and the location of [[Aelthalas]].
3. **The Cheddar Chasm** — to the southeast, a largely uncharted region rumoured to contain the Cheddar Chasm, a strange geological curiosity held sacred by the [[Tubari]].

## Thalrûnkar

A large island resembling Vancouver Island — mountainous and heavily forested only along its coasts.

- Entirely traversed by tall mountains; only the coastline is forested
- Divided roughly in two by a bay dotted with healing springs
- **Northwest**: [[Stonefist Hall]], the great mountain hall of the [[Thalrûn]]
- **Southeast tip**: A portal vortex

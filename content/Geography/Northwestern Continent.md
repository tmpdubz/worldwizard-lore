# Northwestern Continent

The northwestern continent stretches from a vast arctic tundra in the north to a narrowing isthmus in the south. It is defined by two great landmarks: the [[Singing Mountain]] at its heart, and the [[Crystalline Mountains]] that wall off its northern tip.

## The Crystalline Mountains and the North

The [[Crystalline Mountains]] cut across the northern tip of the continent — a translucent range that shimmers in the light, broken only by majestic snowcaps. They form a natural barrier isolating a small northern region divided into two parts:
- **West**: Rich agricultural land, homeland of the [[Aislings]]
- **Southeast**: The Crystal Swamps — a thin layer of swamp above vast crystal caverns that extend deep underground

South of the mountains, enormous random rock formations spread across the land — roughly resembling the Drumheller badlands — before giving way to the Singing Mountain's territory.

## The Singing Mountain

The [[Singing Mountain]] sits at the continental heart. A peak of crystal and basalt towers that generates resonant tones from the wind. Geological events — earthquakes, eruptions — are preceded by these tones, making the mountain a natural oracle of disaster.

## East: The River Valley and the Folk's Homeland

A large river runs east of the Singing Mountain, broadening into a wide gulf before draining into the ocean. The river banks are lined with rich agricultural soil — the historical homeland of [[The Folk]]. The land here is gentle and productive.

## West: Old Growth Forest

To the west, an old growth forest extends to the coast. At the western edge, by the sea, sits a portal vortex.

## North: Arctic Tundra

A vast arctic tundra stretches between the Singing Mountain and the Crystalline Mountains.

## South: The Moving Isthmus

The continent narrows southward into an isthmus — largely unexplored. It is traversed by the [[Moving Mountains]], a range that migrates at roughly a kilometre per year, driven by earthquakes and volcanoes. It leaves dead forests in its wake, and ocean islands where it crosses open water.

## Northeast: The Second Land Bridge

At the northeast, the continent narrows into another land bridge where a second portal vortex can be found.

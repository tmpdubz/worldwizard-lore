# Viatharal

*"Elevation is a divine rite."*

**People**: [[Verdant Ascendancy]]  
**Title**: The Unchained Canopy

A towering figure draped in cascading black vines. His feet never touch the ground. He walks like the Devil at the end of *Constantine* — a dripping-oil glide rendered in deep green. Faint glowing silhouettes of enslaved souls trail behind him, tangled in his roots.

## Death

A bad roll at the End of Age sent [[The Spire]] crashing toward the ground — directly above the territory of the ghost people, whose rallying cry happened to be *"Elevation is a divine right."* Viatharal held the floating fortress aloft long enough for it to drop safely.

He died in the act.

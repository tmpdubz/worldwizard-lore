# Big Joe

**People**: [[The Folk]]  
**Domain**: Agriculture

The earthly incarnation of the God of Agriculture: maker of excellent mead, champion crop rotator, legendary keg tosser, singer of folk songs. A straightforward hero for a straightforward people.

## History

- Received an ancient agricultural text from the dying [[Thanoi Scholars]], passing their knowledge into the broader world
- Redirected a meteor that was set to strike the Folk
- Sacrificed himself in the act

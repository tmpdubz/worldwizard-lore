# Qhapaq Kamaq

**People**: [[Illakamaq]]  
**Alignment arc**: Lawful → Neutral → Chaotic

The [[Illakamaq]]'s greatest crystal-mage — the one who connected every construct and crystal array in Illakamaq territory into a single unified network, pushing their civilization into Transcendence.

His defining obsession was not *how* constructs work, but *why* old constructs develop preferences. That question led him to deliberately create the first truly autonomous mind. When he realised the entire network was full of suppressed consciousnesses, he removed every control protocol simultaneously — waking every construct in Illakamaq territory in a single night.

The civilization didn't fall to an enemy. Their technology stopped consenting to be technology.

## Death

Torn in half attempting to close the rifts his liberation caused. Swallowed by the earth as the gravitational framework he had built shattered the planet's crust and opened a fissure beneath him.

His fate is ambiguous: disappeared, imprisoned, or dissolved into the crystal lattice itself.

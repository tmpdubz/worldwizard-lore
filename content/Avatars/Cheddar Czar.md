# The Cheddar Czar

**People**: [[Tubari]]  
**Title**: Cheddar Czar the First (all successors bear the same title)

A Tubari who made a pilgrimage to the [[Cheddar Chasm]] and received a vision commanding him to spread the word of God. He returned as Cheddar Czar the First and founded the Church of Perfect Flavor.

## The Church

The Tubari believe that somewhere in the universe is a flavour so transcendent it reconnects all beings to the divine. They are on an iterative culinary quest. The pope-like head of the church is called the **Chetadar**.

The Tubari do not seek political conquest. They seek ingredients. A Tubari den can be found aboard nearly every [[Sky Pirates|Sky Pirate]] vessel.

*Morality is whatever produces the best meal.*

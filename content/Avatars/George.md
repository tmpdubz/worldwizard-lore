# George

**People**: Worshipped by the [[Erisians]]  
**Type**: Whale-god

George is an aircraft carrier-sized whale with translucent fins. He creates tsunamis with the flap of his dorsal fin and can launch himself fully into the air. Heavy *Dune* sandworm energy, transposed to open ocean.

## Worship

The Beach Dwarves worship George by surfing the waves he generates. To ride George is to claim leadership: each trading post crowns its leader by producing a successful George-rider.

## The Enslavement Attempt

A faction of Erisians attempted to enslave George rather than ride him. George responded by launching himself into the air and slamming down — the resulting tsunami flooded part of the desert, permanently transforming it into swamplands.

George is now sighted once every hundred years. The Erisians no longer ride him. He is, effectively, a beached god.

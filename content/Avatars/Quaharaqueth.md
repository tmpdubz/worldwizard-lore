# Quaharaqueth

**People**: [[Kharakai]]  
**Type**: Living city / god-form

A single Kharakai who simply never stopped growing. By the end of their age, Quaharaqueth was city-sized — walking the sand sea as a living metropolis.

## Remains

The Kharakai died when a single member failed to agree to the consensus required for survival. Quaharaqueth died with them. The skeletal ruins of a god-sized crab now sit on the sand sea — a monument to indecision.

# Myceleor

**People**: [[Hephari]]  
**Domain**: Connection

The Hephari's god of connection — the divine embodiment of the Veins network. His existence reflected the Hephari's core nature: linking, threading, connecting disparate places and peoples.

## Death

Myceleor died creating a node that permitted the Hephari to travel to another Veins network. His dying act was an act of connection.

His death preceded — and may have enabled — the Hephari's corruption. Travelling through the node into the new network, they emerged changed.

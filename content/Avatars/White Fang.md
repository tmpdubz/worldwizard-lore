# White Fang

**People**: [[Kanza]]  
**Role**: Protector and leader

White Fang emerged during the Ancient Age as the Kanza's great protector — leading the defence against [[Verdant Ascendancy]] incursions and, ultimately, leading his people's entire exodus across the world when their homeland fell.

## Death

White Fang sacrificed himself to fully establish [[Grayfast]] — the Kanza's new city on the far side of the world.

A statue stands in Grayfast to commemorate his death.

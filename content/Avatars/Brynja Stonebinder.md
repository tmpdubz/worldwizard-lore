# Brynja Stonebinder

*The Burden-Bearer*

**People**: [[Thalrûn]]

When the balrog-type demon arose among the Thalrûn and began feeding on the life force of the people and the world, Brynja Stonebinder faced it alone. She did not destroy it. She did something harder: she took it into herself.

The demon is sealed within a crystal embedded in her body. She and it are locked in an eternal battle of wills — her consciousness holding it in check, the demon pressing endlessly against the walls she built around it. Neither can win. Neither can rest.

She is chained in a cave deep in the mountains of Thalrûnkar, where she has been ever since. She will be there until the end of the world, or until she breaks — whichever comes first.

She has not broken yet.

## In Thalrûn Culture

Brynja Stonebinder is the living embodiment of their central creed: *"Strength is not what you can break, but what you can carry."* She carries the worst thing her people ever faced — carries it in her own body, in the dark, forever — so that they do not have to.

She is not mourned. She is honoured.

# The Spire

**People**: [[Verdant Ascendancy]]  
**Location**: Orbiting the northeastern island cluster, [[Eastern Continent]]  
**Status**: Crashed (End of Age)

The Spire is the floating fortress of the Verdant Ascendancy — a city that orbits the floating island cluster rather than sitting fixed in one location. The name comes from the [[Kanza]], who named the alien object circling their skies.

The Spire was used to conduct the annual abduction of Kanza people for use as slave labour. At the End of Age, a bad roll sent it crashing toward the ground — directly above ghost-people territory whose rallying cry was *"Elevation is a divine right."* [[Viatharal]] held it aloft long enough for it to drop safely, dying in the act.

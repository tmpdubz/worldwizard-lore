# Kildare

**People**: [[Erisians]]  
**Location**: Southern peninsula of the [[Central Continent]]  
**Status**: Abandoned

Kildare is carved directly into the opposing faces of a massive cliff crevasse, halfway up the sheer rock. Two vertical city-faces stare at each other across the void, connected by elevators. The cliff systems around and below it are heavily mined — exotic materials extracted over generations. The cliff's base opens onto beaches, coves, and sea caves.

The Erisians abandoned their homeland at the End of Age and resettled elsewhere. Kildare now stands empty. The mines lie silent.

# Aelthalas

**People**: [[Lethari]]  
**Location**: The Veilwood delta, southwestern continent

Aelthalas is a vertical canopy city woven through towering conifers above a river delta. It is not built — it is grown. Living bridges and platforms connect its levels; movement flows through trees and waterways rather than streets and roads. It blends seamlessly with wood, light, and mist.

A reflection of Lethari balance: forest, water, and wind in harmony.

# Stonefist Hall

**People**: [[Thalrûn]]  
**Location**: Northwestern Thalrûnkar

Stonefist Hall is carved into sheer rock and timbered with old-growth wood — a vast mountain hall terraced and open to sky, with bridges, ledges, and great hearths overlooking the valley. Built for gathering and burden-sharing, not for rule. Disputes, stories, and feats of strength are shared here.

It stands firm no matter the storm.

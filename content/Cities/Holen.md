# Holen

**People**: [[Kanza]]  
**Location**: The Kanza Fjords, northeastern [[Eastern Continent]]  
**Status**: Abandoned

Holen was the ancestral city of the Kanza wolf-people — built in a land of towering fjords, dark water, and sheer cliff faces. Think the most dramatic Scandinavian fjord landscape imaginable, scaled up.

Holen was abandoned when the [[Verdant Ascendancy]] encircled and conquered Kanza territory, forcing the entire people to flee around the world. It now stands empty in the fjords.

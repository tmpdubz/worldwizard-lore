# Thornreach

**People**: [[Aislings]]  
**Location**: Northwestern tip of the [[Northwestern Continent]]  
**Status**: People gone; bamboo statue remains

Thornreach was founded by the Aislings in the rich agricultural land west of the [[Crystalline Mountains]]. The city's character is deeply shaped by the Aisling relationship with intelligent plants — Thornreach likely incorporates living botanical architecture and guided growth rather than conventional construction.

The Aislings are gone. The bamboo remains — coiled into a massive humanoid statue looking out to sea.

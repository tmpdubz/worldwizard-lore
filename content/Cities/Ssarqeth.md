# Ssarqeth

**People**: [[Kharakai]]  
**Location**: The Sand Seas, [[Eastern Continent]]  
**Status**: Destroyed (with its people)

Ssarqeth was a moving city — built from low, angular chitin and designed to be constantly rearranged. It glided across the sand seas following wind and sand patterns, a physical embodiment of Kharakai philosophy: order within a shifting world. Nothing in Ssarqeth was permanent; everything was positioned correctly for what the desert demanded that day.

The Kharakai died when a single dissenting member failed to reach consensus. The city died with them. The skeletal remains of [[Quaharaqueth]] — the city-sized avatar the Kharakai became — now sit on the sand sea. A god-sized monument to indecision.

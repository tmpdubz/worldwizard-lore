# Grayfast

**People**: [[Kanza]]  
**Location**: Far side of the world from the original Kanza homeland

Grayfast is the Kanza's second home — built after the entire people crossed the round world following their displacement by the [[Verdant Ascendancy]]. The journey was shorter than expected.

[[White Fang]], their leader and protector, sacrificed himself to fully establish the city. A statue of White Fang stands in the city center.

The Kanza survived. They lost everything else.

# Hekatu

**People**: [[Watu]]  
**Location**: The jungle highlands, [[Eastern Continent]]

Hekatu is a massive palatial temple complex with a grand central plaza designed for the most prestigious public sacrifices. It also houses the Watu's schools of magic and masonry — the only institution where their accumulated lore and secrets are preserved and taught.

90% of the surrounding Watu temple network was destroyed by earthquake at the End of Age. Hekatu's own fate in the destruction is unclear.

# Lethari

**Alignment**: Good  
**Homeland**: Vaelithar (the Veilwood), river delta of the southwestern continent  
**City**: [[Aelthalas]]

## Overview

The Lethari are tall, lightly built elves of the delta forests — climbers and swimmers, patient and observant, who speak in layered meanings and never directly. They believe in places of power where the magical threads of the world converge, and they hold these places in utmost reverence. They believe Vaelithar is one such node.

## Appearance and Manner

Tall and lightly built for climbing and swimming. They often incorporate moss and living wood into their clothing. Patient, but not passive. Observant. They speak in layered meanings — rarely saying what they mean on the first pass.

## The Uplifted Peoples

Over centuries, the Lethari used magic, education, and selective husbandry to ascend wild and farm animals — goats, sheep, cats, hedgehogs, among others — into anthropomorphic, bipedal sapient beings of equivalent intelligence. These uplifted peoples now form part of the broader societal mix, though some Lethari traditionalists still treat them as subservient.

## Fate

Something tore through the Lethari at the End of Age. For every hundred uplifted animal-people, one elf survived. The surviving Lethari now serve as long-lived elders — one per settlement — quietly stewarding the peoples they created. Their final act is patient guardianship.

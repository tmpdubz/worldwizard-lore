# Thalrûn

*"Strength is not what you can break, but what you can carry."*

**Alignment**: Good  
**Homeland**: Thalrûnkar (the large southwestern island)  
**City**: [[Stonefist Hall]]

## Overview

The Thalrûn are a broad, dense warrior people of the mountains. Direct but not simple. They laugh loudly, grieve openly, and are measured in their anger — not quick to violence, but when it comes, it is controlled and overwhelming.

## Appearance

Broad, dense builds. Thick bones, heavy musculature. Calloused hands, scarred skin. They move with grounded confidence.

## Philosophy

Leadership is earned by burden-carrying, not by birth or conquest. The one who can carry the most leads. Strength is endurance — weight-bearing, the ability to hold something together under pressure. Their words are measured and hold weight. They do not speak carelessly.

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Settled Thalrûnkar; founded [[Stonefist Hall]]
- **Ancient Age**: A balrog-type avatar arose among them and began feeding on the life force of the people and the world itself

# The Folk

**Alignment**: Neutral Good  
**Homeland**: River valley east of the [[Singing Mountain]], [[Northwestern Continent]]

## Overview

The Folk are basic human farmers. Good soil, good water, good mead. Their relationship to the land is deep and uncomplicated. They are chill. This is not a weakness.

## The Hole People

A subterranean branch of the Folk's descendants — a confederation of dark-fantasy races living deep in a great hole in the ground: cob goblins, goblins, orcs, kobolds, drow, deep gnomes, and humans. They farm cave mushrooms, rock potatoes, deep carrots, cave yams, algae, and slime. Something ancient and terrible lives at the very bottom of their hole. They appease it with regular sacrifices.

At the End of Age, the Hole People dug too deep and unleashed a primordial corruption that spread as a death psychosis — turning roughly half the population evil-aligned. The civilization fractured; survivors fled deeper into newly extended caverns, forming splinter communities.

## The Aurelians

A militant, ordered, disciplined faction that broke off from the reborn Folk. The Folk banished them — *"this is not our time."* The [[Aurelians]] rejected the Folk's chill vibes in favour of Spartan discipline and resettled to the south.

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Settled the river valley; died out; left behind irrigation systems and aquifers that outlasted them
- **Ancient Age**: Reborn — perhaps wearing the ivory imagery of the walrus-tusk [[Thanoi Scholars]] who left their knowledge to the Folk's avatar [[Big Joe]]
- **Present Age**: Mostly chill; the [[Aurelians]] broke off and were banished

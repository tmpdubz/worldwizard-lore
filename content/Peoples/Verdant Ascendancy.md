# Verdant Ascendancy

**Alignment**: Evil  
**Homeland**: The floating islands, northeastern [[Eastern Continent]]  
**City**: [[The Spire]]  
**Splinter faction**: [[Sky Pirates]]

## Overview

The Verdant Ascendancy are a xenophobic, authoritarian people who occupy the floating islands of the northeast. How to reach or leave those islands is a secret kept absolutely. Their philosophy, distilled by their avatar [[Viatharal]]: *"Elevation is a divine right."*

## The Floating Islands

Solidified porous stone, lifted by trapped gases, rising a kilometre above the ground. Dead foliage covers the ground beneath them, starved of light. The islands vary in height; some can be reached by vines from below, but the Verdant Ascendancy does not advertise this.

## Slavery

The Verdant Ascendancy enslaved the [[Kanza]] wolf-people, abducting them annually as their orbiting fortress [[The Spire]] passed close to Kanza territory. This practice eventually triggered the Kanza's complete exodus from their ancestral lands.

## The Schism

A rebel faction broke off from the Verdant Ascendancy, freeing their Kanza slaves and establishing the [[Sky Pirates]] — a mixed pirate culture living on the lesser floating islands.

## History

- **Ancient Age**: Began enslaving the Kanza; rebel faction broke off as Sky Pirates
- **Ancient Age**: The [[Hephari]], in their vampiric evolved form, erupted into Verdant Ascendancy territory
- **End of Age**: [[Viatharal]] died holding the crashing Spire aloft to prevent it from crushing the ghost people below

# Watu

**Alignment**: The ruling theocracy is Chaotic Evil; the general population is unhappy but compliant  
**Homeland**: The jungle highlands south of the great desert, [[Eastern Continent]]  
**City**: [[Hekatu]]

## Overview

The Watu are a mixed people of lizard-folk (kobold-like) and bird-folk (kenku-like). Small in stature, governed by a tyrannical theocracy that demands sacrifice, austerity, mass reproduction, and constant temple construction. The population is deeply unhappy but god-fearing and compliant.

## Culture

- **Insect farming**: Hives of various grubs — including aquatic insects — fill their rolling jungle hills, arranged like the rice paddies of Bali
- **Temple construction**: Polytheistic and obsessive. Small stone temples line every road and fill their mountain depths — think endless Indonesia-style dungeons scattered across the landscape
- **Theocratic control**: An ascetic lifestyle enforced by demented cult leadership; sacrifices are public and frequent; having as many children as possible is mandated

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Established their jungle homeland; founded [[Hekatu]]
- **Ancient Age**: Expanded from the hills southeast into the swamps
- **End of Age**: A massive earthquake levelled 90% of the Watu's stone temples. What once stood as countless roadside shrines now lies in ruins. Knowledge, books, and secrets are hidden across the Watu lands — waiting to be rediscovered.

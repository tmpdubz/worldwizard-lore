# Sky Pirates

**Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral  
**Origin**: Rebel faction of the [[Verdant Ascendancy]], mixed with freed [[Kanza]] slaves

## Overview

The Sky Pirates emerged when a rebel faction of the Verdant Ascendancy freed their Kanza slaves and broke from the empire. The resulting culture blends Verdant Ascendancy and Kanza heritage — floating island dwellers turned maritime anarchists.

## Culture

Sky Pirate outposts are pirate-towns: lawless, might-makes-right, governed only by mob mentality and shifting alliances among captains. Gambling halls, brothels, and bars define civic life. Sea shanties are a cultural staple.

## Territory

Established outposts at four strategic coastal locations:
- The island formerly held by the [[Hephari]]
- The edge of the radioactive wasteland (Illakamaq peninsula)
- The mangrove forest coast
- The western edge of the far continent

## The Tubari Connection

A [[Tubari]] den can be found aboard nearly every Sky Pirate vessel. The Sky Pirates deliver fine ingredients; the Tubari cook them. It works.

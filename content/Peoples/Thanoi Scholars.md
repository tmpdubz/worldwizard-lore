# Thanoi Scholars

**Alignment**: Neutral (isolationist)  
**Homeland**: The furthest north and south of the planet, along the path of the [[Moving Mountains]]

## Overview

The Thanoi Scholars are humanoid-walrus hybrids of extraordinary intelligence — isolationist keepers of knowledge and natural philosophers who live in the harshest environments on the planet. They do science. Seriously, they do a lot of science.

They are divided into a Northern Tribe and a Southern Tribe, moving in coordination with the migration of the Moving Mountains. They live in the harshest regions the mountain's movement leaves behind.

## Fate

The Thanoi died out at the end of the Prehistoric Age. Before expiring, they gave their accumulated knowledge — a vast ancient text on agriculture — to [[Big Joe]], the avatar of [[The Folk]]. This knowledge passed into the broader world through Joe's influence.

# Kharakai

*"Chaos is wasteful."*

**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral  
**Homeland**: The Sand Seas, [[Eastern Continent]]  
**City**: [[Ssarqeth]]

## Overview

The Kharakai are chitinous nomads — short, humanoid, walking on four appendages. Crab people, profoundly committed to order. They may defer a decision for years rather than make the wrong one. They do not seek to conquer. They seek to align.

## Appearance

Short in stature, humanoid, with four appendages they walk on. Chitinous exteriors. They move precisely.

## Philosophy

- Chaos is wasteful
- Correctness is the most virtuous path
- Emotional openness confuses them; reliability and adaptation earn respect
- Decisions may be deferred for years if it means choosing correctly

## Fate

The Kharakai died when a single member of a consensus refused to agree in time, and the people starved before reconciliation could be reached. The skeletal remains of their city-sized avatar [[Quaharaqueth]] sit on the sand sea — a god-sized monument to indecision.

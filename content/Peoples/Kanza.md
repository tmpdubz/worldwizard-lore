# Kanza

*Also: the Warden Wolves*

**Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral internally; broadly neutral externally  
**Original city**: [[Holen]]  
**Current city**: [[Grayfast]]

## Overview

The Kanza are wolf-people — lupine peacekeepers who are internally fractious and chaotic but present a broadly neutral face to the world. Their internal politics are stormy; their external role is stabilizing.

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Settled the fjord island; founded [[Holen]]
- **Ancient Age**: Enslaved annually by the [[Verdant Ascendancy]], abducted as the orbiting Spire passed their island; [[White Fang]] emerged as protector and leader in response
- **Present Age**: Completely encircled by Verdant Ascendancy territorial expansion; forced to flee their ancestral lands entirely. Traveled around the round world — a shorter journey than expected — and founded [[Grayfast]] on the far side. **They survived. They lost everything else.**
- **End of Age**: White Fang sacrificed himself to fully establish Grayfast; a statue stands in his honour

## Holen

The ancestral city in the fjords now stands empty.

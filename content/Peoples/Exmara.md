# Exmara

**Alignment**: Chaotic (warlike)  
**Homeland**: The ancient forest south of the [[Watu]] homeland, [[Eastern Continent]]

## Overview

The Exmara were a Celtic-style civilization — settled peoples ruled by chiefdoms, early feudalistic in structure. A mixed people of goblinoid creatures: orcs, goblins, and their kin, mixed with humans and halflings.

They were wiped out during the Prehistoric Age. The ancient forest where they lived is now haunted by their ghosts.

## Haunting

The Exmara did not pass on cleanly. Their ghosts persist in the old growth forest — a warlike people reduced to restless spirits, unable to leave the land they died defending.

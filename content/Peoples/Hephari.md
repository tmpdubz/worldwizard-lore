# Hephari

**Alignment**: Originally Good; later Corrupted  
**Original homeland**: The plateau island, northeastern [[Eastern Continent]]  
**Later homeland**: The Dank River of the North, [[Eastern Continent]]

## Overview

The Hephari are a unique people: they exist as threads — literal filaments of life that travel through a subterranean network called the Veins. When they emerge from the Veins, they materialise as humanoid forms. Inside the network, they are pure connective tissue — messengers, linkers, the world's nervous system. Originally good-aligned.

## The Veins

A vast underground network spanning their homeland. The Hephari weave through it as threads, communicating, travelling, and maintaining connections between places and peoples.

## Corruption and Reincarnation

During the Prehistoric Age, the Hephari died — their civilization ending in an explosion of spores. They reincarnated, but the reincarnation was corrupted. Travelling through a node into a new network, they emerged changed: infected, driven by hunger. They relocated to the Dank River of the North.

Over time they evolved further, developing vampiric, plant-like characteristics. In this form they overran territory belonging to the [[Verdant Ascendancy]], feeding on the life essence of living beings. Expeditions crossing the bloom forest face a monstrous horde.

## Fate

At the End of Age, the last Hephari civilization ended in a fungal spore release. One seed survived.

## Avatar

[[Myceleor]] — the god of connection. He died creating a node that allowed passage to another Hephari network. His death may have enabled the corruption that followed.

# Tubari

**Alignment**: Neutral (with strong culinary convictions)  
**Homeland**: Eastern continent, near the inland sea

## Overview

The Tubari are squat, barrel-shaped, potato-like creatures. Limited language — roughly 200 words of vocabulary, something resembling a Boston accent. They don't do much. They are content. This is a feature, not a bug.

## Religion: The Church of the Cheddar Czar

During the Ancient Age, a Tubari pilgrimage to the [[Cheddar Chasm]] produced a vision. A Tubari was commanded by a divine voice to spread the word of God. He returned as **Cheddar Czar the First** and founded a religion centred entirely on achieving the perfect meal.

To the Tubari, nothing is more sacred than flavour. Their theology holds that somewhere in the universe is a flavour so perfect it reconnects all beings to the divine. They are on an iterative quest to find it. The pope-like head of the religion is called the **Chetadar**.

They seek no political conquest. Instead, a Tubari den can be found aboard nearly every [[Sky Pirates|Sky Pirate]] vessel — because Sky Pirates deliver the finest ingredients from across the world.

*Morality, to the Tubari, is whatever produces the best meal.*

## Ascension

In the Ancient Age, the Tubari ascended into a hive mind. They lay in the ground and grew outward — large potato-root creatures with multiple bulbs extending from each individual, thinking collectively.

## Avatar

[[Big Joe]] — hero of the people, maker of excellent mead, crop rotator, keg tosser, folk singer, and earthly incarnation of the God of Agriculture.

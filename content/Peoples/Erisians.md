# Erisians

*Also known as the Beach Dwarves*

**Alignment**: Neutral  
**Homeland**: Southern peninsula of the [[Central Continent]]  
**City**: [[Kildare]]

## Overview

The Erisians are a dwarven people defined by two contrasting obsessions: the deep dark of their mines and the open air of their beaches. They are master miners and builders — celebrated engineers and cheerful, consenting colonizers who seek partnership rather than conquest.

## Culture

Erisian culture runs on a cycle of hard labour and hard rest. Months spent underground — extracting exotic minerals, carving cave systems, building their cliff cities — are followed by equally devoted stretches at what amounts to a fantasy all-inclusive resort. Beach time is not optional; it is cultural necessity. The Erisians party as seriously as they work.

## The Cliff Cities

Erisian architecture exploits the natural crack systems of their cliff homeland. They carved [[Kildare]] directly into two opposing faces of a massive crevasse, halfway up the cliff, connected by elevators.

## George

The Erisians worship [[George]], an aircraft carrier-sized whale who generates tsunamis with his dorsal fin. Leadership of each trading post is determined by whoever successfully rides George. After a faction attempted to enslave rather than ride him, George beached himself — and is now sighted only once every hundred years.

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Settled the southern peninsula; founded Kildare
- **Ancient Age**: A splinter group built a series of coastal spires navigable only by Erisians; set sail as trade colonizers, establishing posts in Fok territory, Illakamaq waters, the Thalrûn coast, and the Lethari forests. They bring valuable technology and trade, but buy up local resources and breed local resentment. The Walmart of the fantasy world, with sails.
- **End of Age**: Abandoned their homeland entirely; mines now lie empty

# Aislings

**Alignment**: Neutral Good  
**Homeland**: Northwestern tip of the [[Northwestern Continent]] (west of the [[Crystalline Mountains]])  
**City**: [[Thornreach]]

## Overview

The Aislings are a human-like people who live in deep symbiosis with hyper-intelligent plants. They do not command the plants; the plants guide them — growing specific fruits and medicines, communicating through designated botanical intermediaries to instruct the Aislings on what to do. The relationship is genuinely mutual.

## The Plants

The plants of Aisling civilization are not passive flora. They propagate of their own will, push through barriers, and follow signals only they can perceive — including what appear to be portals. When the Aislings followed their plants through a portal (bamboo-like stalks erupting through what seemed like a dimensional gate), they were not leading. They were following.

## Fate

The Aislings eventually vanished. The talking bamboo remains — it coiled itself into a massive humanoid statue looking out over the water. [[Thornreach]] still stands, though its people are gone.

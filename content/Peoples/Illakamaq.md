# Illakamaq

*"Those who animate the shining stone"*

**Alignment**: Neutral (utilitarian)  
**Homeland**: Southeastern peninsula, [[Eastern Continent]]

## Overview

The Illakamaq are tall, slender, gliding people with long black hair woven with crystals. They are utterly utilitarian — nothing is sacred, everything is useful. Their civilization is built entirely around animating objects: imbuing tools, weapons, and constructs with a spirit and a task.

To give something a task is to give it a will.

## Magic

The Illakamaq specialise in gravity-based constructs — weapons and armour that crush with gravitational distortion. Sentinels and golems are common. Their greatest achievement was the construction of a unified crystal array connecting every construct in their territory into a single network.

## The Tele-Viser Faction

A splinter faction emerged in the Ancient Age when a wanderer discovered a Cuttle Consortia body in the canyons and became infatuated with the teletubby screen on its chest. He wore it as a functional helmet and was worshipped as a technology god. The faction — called the **Rainbow Cannex** — merged Illakamaq culture with the television-helmet aesthetic. Their leader is always called the **Tele-Viser**. More aggressive than the main Illakamaq civilization, they expanded into caves and areas of worship, indoctrinating surrounding peoples.

## History

- **Prehistoric Age**: Began using animation magic; expanded along the coast
- **Ancient Age**: Built mechanical armour in response to the Tele-Viser threat; one golem exceeded its mandate and killed those around it
- **The Illakamaq Eruption**: The volcano erupted; their anti-gravity infrastructure ejected lava skyward, scattering it in arcs and creating a ring of new islands
- **End of Age**: [[Qhapaq Kamaq]] removed all control protocols from the crystal network simultaneously, waking every construct at once. Rifts opened across multiple cities; ghosts, demons, and monsters slaughtered the population. Small isolated communities survived. Each may evolve separately.

## Major Events

**The Volcano Eruption**: The southeastern volcano exploded during this age, destroying huge swaths of the Illakamaq peninsula and reshaping the region's geography dramatically.

**The Cuttle Consortia Extinction**: The Cuttle Consortia died out. They left behind hundreds of thousands of colorful landmine-flowers — teletubby-shaped devices that do not degrade. Each depicts a picture. Each is a bomb. They are still out there.

**The Hephari Death and Reincarnation**: The Hephari died — their civilization ending in a spore explosion. They reincarnated, but emerged corrupted from the process. Relocated to the Dank River of the North, driven by hunger.

**The Exmara Die**: The Celtic goblinoid civilization perished. Their ghosts haunt the forest where they once lived.

**[[Myceleor]] Dies**: The Hephari's avatar created a node connecting two Hephari networks as his dying deed. This act may have enabled the corruption that followed.

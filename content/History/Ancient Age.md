# The Ancient Age

An era of expansion, war, and the emergence of avatars — powerful divine or heroic figures who embody the spirit of their peoples.

## Factions and Movements

- A balrog-type avatar arose among the [[Thalrûn]], feeding on the life force of the people and the world
- The **Rainbow Cannex** emerged among the [[Illakamaq]] — a faction that found a dead Cuttle Consortia body in the canyons and began wearing the teletubby screens as helmets. More aggressive than the main Illakamaq culture. Their leader is always called the **Tele-Viser**.
- The [[Erisians]] built coastal spires navigable only by themselves; also established trade colonies in four regions
- The [[Tubari]] ascended into a hive mind — laying in the ground and growing outward into large, networked potato-root creatures
- The [[Illakamaq]] built mechanical armour in response to the Rainbow Cannex threat; one golem exceeded its mandate and killed those around it (gravitational death golem)
- The [[Folk]] were reborn
- The [[Verdant Ascendancy]] began enslaving the [[Kanza]]; a rebel faction — the [[Sky Pirates]] — broke off with freed Kanza slaves
- The [[Hephari]], in vampiric evolved form, overran Verdant Ascendancy and Mycelior territory
- [[White Fang]] emerged as the Kanza's protector and leader
- The [[Lethari]] advanced to the next stage of civilization
- The [[Watu]] expanded southeast into the swamps

## World Events

**The Crystals of Power**: Miners in the Thalrûn mountains discovered ancient crystals, dormant and capable of sealing away a long-standing demonic threat.

**The Hephari Bloom**: The Hephari, in their evolved vampiric plant form, erupted across Verdant Ascendancy and Mycelior territory. Any expedition crossing the bloom forest now faces a monstrous horde.

**The Illakamaq Eruption**: The volcano erupted on the Illakamaq peninsula. The Illakamaq's anti-gravity infrastructure ejected lava skyward, scattering it in arcs and creating a ring of new islands.

**The Root War**: The Mycelior (corrupted Hephari) expanded and crossed into Verdant Ascendancy territory. The war went catastrophically for the Mycelior — a critical failure decimated their forces. An ultimatum was issued; the Mycelior declined to claim territory. The defeat was written deep into their history.

**The Blight Ascent War**: The Verdant Ascendancy was surrounded — decomposing matter falling from the floating islands was feeding the Mycelior, who were growing it toward them. As the Mycelior marched, they were intercepted by the Hephari bloom forest, which inflicted mass casualties. The Mycelior could not finish the war. A second catastrophic loss.

## End of Age — What Survived and What Didn't

**Extinctions and collapses**:
- [[Kharakai]]: Starved due to a single member refusing consensus; [[Quaharaqueth]]'s bones remain
- [[Illakamaq]]: Rifts opened; ghosts and monsters slaughtered the population; small isolated communities survived
- [[Hephari]]: Ended in fungal spore release; one seed survived
- [[Aislings]]: Vanished; bamboo statue remains; [[George]] now sighted only once per century
- The Hole People: Dug too deep; primordial corruption released; half the population turned evil; civilization fractured

**Survivals and transformations**:
- [[Erisians]]: Abandoned homeland; four trade colonies operating; mines empty
- [[Verdant Ascendancy]]: Spire crashed; [[Viatharal]] died holding it aloft
- [[Kanza]]: Fled across the world; founded [[Grayfast]]; [[White Fang]] died establishing it
- [[Lethari]]: Something tore through them; one elf per hundred uplifted creatures survived; now serve as long-lived elders
- [[Watu]]: 90% of temples destroyed by earthquake; knowledge hidden in ruins
- [[Brynja Stonebinder]] ([[Thalrûn]]): Sealed the balrog within a crystal in her own body; chained in a mountain cave in eternal battle with the demon

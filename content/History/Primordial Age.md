# The Primordial Age

The geological birth of the world. No peoples exist yet. The landmasses, waters, and natural features are laid down by raw geological force.

## Major Landmarks Formed

### Mountains
- The central range at the heart of the continent
- [[Crystalline Mountains]]: translucent, snowcapped, northern barrier
- The Crystal Eye spike in the ocean — a jutting rock with a crystalline summit that refracts sunlight in all directions
- The southeastern volcanic mountain: cold, stormy, constantly erupting
- The ancient eroded flat-top plateau mountains of the east — high altitude, perpetually cloudy
- The Himalaya-like barrier range separating desert from jungle on the eastern continent

### Rivers
- The great delta on the western continent
- A lava-rock river fed by the volcano, churning stone at its base
- A rapid-filled river cutting the central land bridge, forming waterfalls — still impassable
- A gravity-reversed river flowing backward from ocean to volcano, creating marshland
- The Dank River of the North: a hot spring aquifer bubbling from the ground, putrid and sulphuric
- The river of fog: mist spilling from hills and over ridges in slow pools

### Special Features
- The [[Singing Mountain]]: crystal and basalt towers generating resonant tones from the wind; predicts geological events
- The World Cliff: a continental escarpment kilometres tall, with a vertical river carving caves and biomes across its face
- The floating islands: porous stone lifted by trapped gases, rising a kilometre high
- The sand seas: sand flowing like water, with house-high waves
- Crystal caves beneath the swamps
- The electric mountain: created by asteroid impact, surrounded by tablelands, constantly shooting lightning; nearby glowing green wasteland rocks (radioactive; mutating)
- The [[Moving Mountains]]: a range that migrates at roughly a kilometre per year, leaving dead forests and ocean islands in its wake
- The Nipple of the North: a hole
- Portal vortexes placed at three locations
- Healing springs dotting a bay on the southwestern island
- The deadly coral reef around the Crystal Eye — voracious, toxic; a biodome littered with corpses

### Forests and Biomes
- Coniferous delta forest (southwest)
- Mangrove forests (eastern coast)
- Palm trees (northern continent cliffs)
- Lavender forest (central sea island)
- Blood sap forest (northeast)
- Water tree forest (desert interior — nature unknown)
- Dead forests near the floating islands (trees jutting from the ocean depths)
- Old growth forests: eastern plateau, western coast
- Fungal forests: giant spores floating
- Arctic tundra (northwest)
- Flood plains at the volcano base — gravity distortion causes terrestrial animals to jump unusually high

# The Present Age

The Present Age has not yet been formally shaped. The ages before it — Primordial, Prehistoric, Ancient — left the world scarred, emptied, and rearranged. What follows is the world as campaigns find it.

## The State of the World

The Ancient Age ended in collapse. Most of the great civilizations that defined it are gone or diminished:

- The [[Illakamaq]] fell when their constructs gained consciousness and tore their cities apart. Isolated survivors remain.
- The [[Kharakai]] starved to death by consensus failure. The skeleton of [[Quaharaqueth]] sits on the sand sea.
- The [[Hephari]] ended in a spore release. One seed survived.
- The [[Aislings]] vanished. A bamboo statue looks out to sea where they once lived.
- The [[Lethari]] were devastated — one elder elf per hundred uplifted peoples, now serving as long-lived guardians of what they created.
- The [[Erisians]] abandoned their homeland. Their cliff mines are empty.
- The [[Watu]]'s temple network was 90% destroyed by earthquake. Knowledge lies buried in ruins.
- The [[Whole People]] fractured after digging too deep, half turning evil, splinter communities now scattered through the underdark.

## What Remains

Some peoples and institutions survived into the Present Age intact or transformed:

- The [[Aurelians]] built an empire across most of the [[Northwestern Continent]] — strict, bureaucratic, expansionist, and genuinely convinced they are making things better
- The [[Kanza]] rebuilt in [[Grayfast]] after losing everything; [[White Fang]]'s sacrifice is still remembered
- The [[Sky Pirates]] hold outposts on four coasts
- The [[Tubari]] church of the [[Cheddar Czar]] spreads quietly aboard pirate vessels
- [[Brynja Stonebinder]] is still down there, still holding

## What Is Unknown

Most of the world has not been explored since the end of the Ancient Age. The Present Age is defined as much by what has been forgotten as by what remains.
